## Acceptance verdict

**accepted-with-open-items**, criteria **declared** (the eight stories' acceptance criteria in `epics.md`).

- Evidence for "met": the per-story table above; a fresh green `npm run check` (1314 unit tests) and 442 browser tests on Chrome and Edge; the 3D chunk under its budget.
- Why not plain "accepted": the 2.4 criterion "30 fps or better on a mid-range phone" is unmeasured; four stories had no independent reviewer and 2.8 had none for a reason outside the work; CI has not run the epic's last four commits and does not run three of the gates the epic depends on; the architecture and format documents have drifted from the build.
- Why not "rejected": no criterion is shown to fail, no blocking finding stands unresolved, and every open item is tracked as an action item or a deferred entry with an owner. The machine verdict was **rejected** only because all eight stories were still `review` when the retro started; the owner overrode it and closed the stories.
