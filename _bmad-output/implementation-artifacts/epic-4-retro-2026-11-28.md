## Findings

### Behavior check (screenshots of the 14 fixture topics in English and Urdu)

- **B1. The surface fixtures look wrong in Tier A.** The axes box is a cube (`range` for x, y and z), so a surface that is tall and narrow is squeezed: `sf-saddle` (x·y over -3..3, z up to ±9, box ±10) draws a thin spike, and `sf-paraboloid` (-2..2, z to 8, box 10) is a small bowl in a big box (screenshots `saddleTopic-ur`, `bowlTopic-en`). The numbers are right; the picture does not teach the shape. Cause: spec 4.5 has one `range`; the unit tests could not see it. Disposition: **fix** (action item 42). Not fixed here because it changes the schema (a separate `zRange`) and the fixtures' known values should stay as they are.
- **B2. Cube labels overlap.** `e1`, `e2`, `e3` sit on top of each other near the origin for the identity matrix (`cubeTopic-en`). Cosmetic; the text list carries the same information. Disposition: accept.
- **B3. Everything else looked right:** the Argand diagram in Urdu (axis names, `r =` and `θ =` readable, picture left to right), Riemann slices with the area label, the limit graph in Urdu, the F on the grid with e1 and e2, the eigen grid in Urdu. The things the Epic 5 retro found only by looking (right-to-left text anchors, Urdu line heights) did not recur: the new scenes draw their text through the shared renderers fixed there. Disposition: accept.

### Diff-scope review (blind hunter, edge-case hunter, verification-gap reviewer)

About 55 items came back. Re-checked against the code; fixed in `e416970`, with tests:

- **R1.** `slopeAt` rejected a steep smooth curve (1000x at 0) as "a corner or a jump" (three reviewers; `src/lib/graph/calculus.ts`). Rewritten to compare one-sided slopes at two distances: 1000x has a tangent, `|x|`, `sign(x)` and the cube root at 0 have none. The surface probe inherits the fix.
- **R2.** `limitOf` called `f(x) = x` with h of 2 "no limit", and `1/x^2` a finite limit. Each side is now extrapolated to the point and a value over 1e6 is no finite limit.
- **R3.** `MatrixViewer` made a WebGL context for a 2D matrix that never uses it. `useTier(enabled)` skips the probe.
- **R4.** Surface: a window wholly beyond the reach was inverted instead of defaulted; "1 points" (plural), a clamped probe not said in words; a negative zero shown as `−0` in three viewers; a determinant that overflows to Infinity now leaves the scene out.
- **R5.** Verification gap: no test showed Tier A mounting a canvas for the cube or a surface (the "Tier A loads three.js" test passed even if `Boundary` dropped the viewer to Tier C). Added e2e tests (tier A, ready, one canvas, no page error) in `matrix.spec.ts` and `surface.spec.ts`.
- **Refuted:** "no tests exist for the new modules", "unused imports in `SolidViewer`", "other `Program` mocks lack `fn2`" (the reviewers saw only the source diff; typecheck, lint and 2,108 unit tests pass).
- **Deferred** (`deferred-work.md`, four new entries): picture symbols (`r =`, `λ =`, `P`) and `nameOf` take the first language, not the visitor's; the derivative graph and the tangent disagree at a corner; vague skip reasons (riemann with from >= to, limitProbe with h <= 0, bad `n`, op that names a power); shared ids across shapes of one element; LOOKS repeat after 8 of the 24 elements `space3d` allows; the unreachable 3D `volumeChange` sentence; no axes, floor or box toggles in the matrix and surface viewers; a topic parameter named `x` or `y` is shadowed by `fn2`.
