Visual Math: five color theme variations

Each variation shows light and dark mode with a 360px lesson snippet in English (left-to-right) and Urdu (right-to-left text panel; scene and formula stay left-to-right). Correct and wrong feedback always pair an icon and a word with colour (solid border plus check for correct, dashed border plus cross for wrong). Contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x relative luminance; body text target is 4.5:1.

Variations are presented in no particular order. Fonts are the system stack; Urdu uses Noto Nastaliq Urdu or Jameel Noor Nastaleeq if installed, otherwise a system fallback.
